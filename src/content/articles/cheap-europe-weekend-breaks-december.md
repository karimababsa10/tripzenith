<h1>12 Cheap Weekend Breaks in Europe for December Under €150</h1>
<p>Alright, mate! Let’s talk about some delightful weekend escapes in Europe this December. You’re probably dreaming of a break without burning a hole in your pocket, right? As someone who’s gallivanted across 60+ countries, I can promise you that Europe brims with charming destinations perfect for a winter adventure. So, grab yourself a cuppa, and let’s dive into these 12 cheap weekend breaks where you can enjoy the festive cheer—all for under €150.</p>

<h2>1. Budapest, Hungary</h2>
<p>Imagine gazing at the beautifully twinkling lights along the Danube River, enjoying a warm cup of mulled wine in one hand. December is magical in Budapest, thanks to its renowned Christmas markets.</p>
<p><strong>Average Costs:</strong> Flight and accommodation can often be found for around €100. A hearty meal at a local eatery costs about €10.</p>
<p><strong>Must-See:</strong> The festive market at Vörösmarty Square is a must, along with the stunning St. Stephen's Basilica, where you can enjoy carol concerts.</p>

<h2>2. Prague, Czech Republic</h2>
<p>Ah, the fairytale city of Prague! Cobblestone streets, gothic architecture, and a delightful Christmas market at the Old Town Square will cast a spell on you.</p>
<p><strong>Average Costs:</strong> Flights and hotels can be secured for under €120. Enjoy a lavish meal for around €12.</p>
<p><strong>Tips:</strong> Grab a trdelník (a sweet pastry) at the market—pure bliss!</p>

<h2>3. Kraków, Poland</h2>
<p>Kraków is one of those cities that gets bustling with energy in December. With its medieval charm, it’s an awesome place to stroll and soak in the holiday atmosphere.</p>
<p><strong>Average Costs:</strong> You can find flights and accommodation for about €110. Meals can be enjoyed for under €10.</p>
<p><strong>Things to Do:</strong> Be sure to visit the Wawel Castle and try the local pierogi!</p>

<h2>4. Tallinn, Estonia</h2>
<p>Picture a winter wonderland: snow-laden rooftops and a cozy atmosphere in the Tallinn old town. Estonia’s capital is another beautiful place to spend your December weekend.</p>
<p><strong>Average Costs:</strong> You can get around €130 for the full experience, including flights and hotel. A delicious meal will cost about €13.</p>
<p><strong>Highlight:</strong> The Christmas market in the town square is enchanting and perfect for some beautiful evening photos.</p>

<h2>5. Lisbon, Portugal</h2>
<p>While many think of Lisbon in summer, December is surprisingly delightful—with fewer tourists and cooler temperatures.</p>
<p><strong>Average Costs:</strong> You can book a round-trip flight and accommodation for about €140. Enjoy a meal for roughly €15.</p>
<p><strong>Activities:</strong> Don’t miss the historical Alfama district and try a traditional pastel de nata.</p>

<h2>6. Brussels, Belgium</h2>
<p>If chocolate and beer are your happy places, Brussels is calling! December transforms Brussels with enchanting lights and festive themes.</p>
<p><strong>Average Costs:</strong> Flights and accommodation can be squeezed for around €130. A meal will typically set you back €14.</p>
<p><strong>Recommendation:</strong> Explore the Winter Wonders market, and indulge in some Belgian chocolates!</p>

<h2>7. Bucharest, Romania</h2>
<p>Bucharest blends history with modern vibes, and it’s quite affordable too. The Christmas market at University Square is a vibrant experience.</p>
<p><strong>Average Costs:</strong> Expect to spend around €120 for the weekend, including flights. Local meals are about €10.</p>
<p><strong>Must-Visit:</strong> The Palace of Parliament is a remarkable sight, just don’t forget your camera!</p>

<h2>8. Seville, Spain</h2>
<p>Seville in winter? Yes, please! Enjoy milder temperatures while checking out incredible historical sites like the Alcázar.</p>
<p><strong>Average Costs:</strong> Flights and accommodation can hover around €150.</p>
<p><strong>Iconic Dish:</strong> You can feast on tapas for about €12. Don’t skip the local churros!</p>

<h2>9. Edinburgh, Scotland</h2>
<p>Edinburgh during December is like stepping straight into a Christmas card. The festive atmosphere, paired with its stunning architecture, truly enchants visitors.</p>
<p><strong>Average Costs:</strong> Flights and accommodations can typically fit within €150. Traditional meals go for around €15.</p>
<p><strong>Experience:</strong> Explore the Christmas market and hike up to Arthur's Seat for breathtaking views.</p>

<h2>10. Dublin, Ireland</h2>
<p>The Irish capital buzzes with warmth and cheer in December. The Guinness Storehouse is a fun stop after enjoying the festive decorations around the city.</p>
<p><strong>Average Costs:</strong> Usually, you can expect to spend about €140 for the whole weekend.</p>
<p><strong>Culinary Treats:</strong> Sample some good Irish stew for about €12 at a local pub!</p>

<h2>11. Riga, Latvia</h2>
<p>Riga’s city center is an architectural gem that becomes even more stunning in December. The Christmas market in the old town is definitely worth wandering through.</p>
<p><strong>Average Costs:</strong> Flights and hotel can be found for around €130, with meals costing about €9.</p>
<p><strong>Don't Miss:</strong> Visit the House of Blackheads—it's simply picturesque!</p>

<h2>12. Malta</h2>
<p>While it's not the first place one thinks of for December, Malta presents mild temperatures and a unique holiday experience.</p>
<p><strong>Average Costs:</strong> A weekend in Malta might run about €150, flights included. Meals typically cost around €15.</p>
<p><strong>Website Suggestions:</strong> Explore the charming Mdina and catch a glimpse of stunning architecture at the St. John’s Co-Cathedral.</p>

<h2>Things to Consider</h2>
<p>Now, let's keep it real. While the budget of €150 includes flights and accommodation for many of these destinations, don't forget to budget for food, activities, and souvenirs. Prices can fluctuate, so it’s always best to keep an eye out for deals. Traveling in winter is not only budget-friendly but also allows you to experience unique charm and spirit during the festive season.</p>
<p>Sure, not every trip will stay under budget, but with some savvy planning, you can keep your costs down while reveling in the seasonal magic Europe has to offer.</p>

<p>So, there you have it—12 fantastic weekend getaways that'll let you embrace the holiday spirit without breaking the bank. December is a beautiful time to travel, and I hope this inspires your next adventure. Safe travels, and keep exploring!</p>
