<h3>November Weather</h3>
<p>Expect mild temperatures ranging from 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).</p>
<h3>Approximate Budget</h3>
<p>Around €600–€800 for a week, including accommodation, meals, and activities.</p>
<h3>Why Visit in November</h3>
<p>The crowds are significantly lower than in summer, and you can enjoy incredible natural landscapes.</p>

<ul>
<li>Visit Teide National Park</li>

<li>Relax at Playa de las Américas</li>
<li>Discover the local cuisine at Santa Cruz markets</li>
</ul>
<h3>Advantages and Disadvantages</h3>
<strong>Advantages:</strong> Year-round sun and diverse activities.<br>
<strong>Disadvantages:</strong> International flights can be pricey.
