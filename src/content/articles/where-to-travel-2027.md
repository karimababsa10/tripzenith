<h1>Where to Travel in 2027: 15 Destinations to Book Early</h1>

<h2>Introduction</h2>
<p>As a seasoned traveler who's adventured through over 60 countries, I often find myself dreaming of future escapades. Planning travel for 2027 might seem premature, but trust me, the best experiences come to those who prepare in advance. So, grab a cup of tea, sit back, and let’s explore 15 destinations worth booking early!</p>

<h2>1. Japan</h2>
<p>The Land of the Rising Sun is buzzing with excitement. With the anticipated opening of new attractions and cultural festivals, 2027 is shaping up to be a fantastic year.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> Spring (March to May) for cherry blossoms and autumn (September to November) for stunning foliage.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Flights may range from £600-£800, and hotels can start around £100 per night.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Don’t miss experiencing the vibrant cherry blossom festivals or riding Japan’s famous Shinkansen (bullet train) across the country.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Early-bird discounts on flights and accommodations can save you a significant amount, especially during peak seasons.</li>
</ul>

<h2>2. Portugal</h2>
<p>Portugal’s charm is undeniable, with its stunning coastline, historic cities, and delectable cuisine. It’s been gaining popularity, and for good reason.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> Late spring and early autumn (April to June, September to October) are perfect for pleasant weather and fewer crowds.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Flights from the UK can start around £100, and basic accommodations range from £50-£150 per night.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Explore the picturesque streets of Lisbon, savoring pastel de nata in one of the charming cafés, or stroll along the stunning cliffs of the Algarve.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Booking your trip early could mean better deals on flights, especially around summer.</li>
</ul>

<h2>3. South Africa</h2>
<p>Renowned for its diverse landscapes and rich wildlife, South Africa is a destination that should be on every adventurer’s bucket list.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> May to September, which is winter in South Africa and an ideal time for wildlife viewing.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Expect flights to be around £500-£700, with mid-range accommodations averaging £70-£120.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> A safari in Kruger National Park is a must. You might even join a guided walking safari for an up-close encounter with nature.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Wildlife tour operators often provide early booking discounts, so get in early for the best deals.</li>
</ul>

<h2>4. New Zealand</h2>
<p>With majestic landscapes, vibrant cities, and thrilling outdoor activities, New Zealand offers something for everyone.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> November to March, during their summer months.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Flights can be expensive, ranging from £800-£1,200. Accommodation starts around £80 per night.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Visit the filming locations of The Lord of the Rings for an epic adventure, or trek the Tongariro Alpine Crossing for stunning views.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Early booking for tours can secure both spots and lower prices, especially for popular activities like bungee jumping.</li>
</ul>

<h2>5. Greece</h2>
<p>With its sun-drenched islands and rich history, Greece continues to be a beloved destination that promises beauty and culture.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> Late spring and early fall (May, September) are ideal to avoid the heat and crowds.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Flights average around £200, and island accommodations can range from £60-£180.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Explore the ancient ruins of Athens, followed by a ferry to the islands. Enjoy the sunset in Santorini or the beaches of Mykonos.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Booking ferry tickets in advance can save you time and hassle during peak months.</li>
</ul>

<h2>6. Canada</h2>
<p>From urban sophistication to staggering natural beauty, Canada is a land of diverse experiences.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> The summer months of June to August offer the best conditions for exploring national parks.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Flights from the UK start at about £500. Accommodations vary widely, averaging £80-£200.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Experience the Calgary Stampede if you visit in July or hike in the stunning Banff National Park.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Early booking for national park passes and camping spots is essential, especially during peak season.</li>
</ul>

<h2>7. Iceland</h2>
<p>With its surreal landscapes, hot springs, and the Northern Lights, Iceland remains a popular destination for adventurers.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> Late autumn and early winter (October to March) for the Northern Lights, or summer months for full exploration.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Flights average around £300-£500, and guesthouses can start at £80 per night.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Take a dip in the Blue Lagoon or hike on a glacier for an unparalleled adventure.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Accommodation prices skyrocket during the Northern Lights season, so booking early is crucial.</li>
</ul>

<h2>8. Vietnam</h2>
<p>Vietnam has been on the rise as a travel hot spot, offering stunning landscapes, rich history, and fascinating culture.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> October to April for cooler, dry weather, especially in the north.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Flights typically cost around £400-£600, while accommodations can be found for as little as £15-£50 per night.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Explore Ha Long Bay on a cruise, or discover the street food culture in Ho Chi Minh City.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Book internal flights well in advance to save money on travel within the country.</li>
</ul>

<h2>9. Croatia</h2>
<p>With stunning coastlines and historical towns, Croatia is becoming a favorite for travelers seeking both relaxation and adventure.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> May to October when the weather is warm, but consider the shoulder months of May and September for fewer crowds.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Flights start at around £150, with hotels costing between £50-£200.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Visit the ancient city of Dubrovnik or explore the beautiful national parks.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Early bookings for accommodations in Dubrovnik can secure lower prices as summer gets closer.</li>
</ul>

<h2>10. Peru</h2>
<p>Home of the ancient Inca civilization, Peru is a destination rich in history and natural beauty.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> The dry season (May to September) is ideal for trekking.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Flights are around £600-£800, with basic accommodations starting at £30-£100.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Trek to Machu Picchu for an unforgettable adventure through the Andes.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Popular treks often require permits, which can sell out quickly, so secure your spot well in advance.</li>
</ul>

<h2>11. Australia</h2>
<p>From the Great Barrier Reef to vibrant cities, Australia has something for everyone, and it’s a fantastic long-haul destination.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> December to February for summer, or June to August for winter sports in the south.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Flights can range from £800-£1,200. Mid-range accommodations often cost £80-£200 per night.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Dive in the Great Barrier Reef or explore the stunning beaches of Sydney.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Booking multi-city flights early can offer significant savings for those planning to explore several areas.</li>
</ul>

<h2>12. Argentina</h2>
<p>With a mix of vibrant cities, breathtaking landscapes, and rich culture, Argentina is incredibly diverse.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> September to November (spring) or March to May (autumn).</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Flights typically hover around £600-£800, and accommodation varies between £50-£150.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Experience tango in Buenos Aires or marvel at the stunning Iguazu Falls.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Booking during off-peak periods can lead to cheaper flights and accommodations.</li>
</ul>

<h2>13. Malaysia</h2>
<p>With a mix of modern cities and stunning natural beauty, Malaysia offers an exciting travel experience.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> December to February when the weather is at its best.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Flights are around £400-£600, and accommodations can start as low as £20.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Explore the lush rainforests of Borneo or the iconic Petronas Twin Towers in Kuala Lumpur.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Early bookings for eco-tours can help secure your spot in popular natural attractions.</li>
</ul>

<h2>14. Turkey</h2>
<p>A bridge between Europe and Asia, Turkey offers fascinating history and stunning landscapes.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> April to June, and September to November for pleasant temperatures.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Expect flights to be around £200-£400, with budget accommodations starting at £30.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Visit the ancient ruins of Ephesus or take a hot air balloon ride over Cappadocia.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Book early for hot air balloon experiences, as these spots fill quickly!</li>
</ul>

<h2>15. UAE (Abu Dhabi and Dubai)</h2>
<p>More than just a shopping destination, the UAE is packed with attractions and exciting experiences.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> November to March, when temperatures are more comfortable.</li>
<li><strong>Expected Costs:</strong> Flights can range from £400-£800, with hotels from £50-£250.</li>
<li><strong>Unique Experience:</strong> Experience the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque in Abu Dhabi or the exhilarating theme parks in Dubai.</li>
<li><strong>Booking Tip:</strong> Look for deals on packages that include flights and hotels, as booking early can bring significant savings.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Conclusion</h2>
<p>Planning for a trip in 2027 might seem like a long way off, but those who act early often secure the best deals on travel, accommodations, and experiences. From the bustling streets of Japan to the tranquil beaches of Croatia, there's a wealth of opportunities waiting. So, dust off your suitcase, jot down your bucket list, and let the planning begin—adventure awaits!</p>
